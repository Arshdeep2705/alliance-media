Alliance Media · Melbourne

The studio

An Australian house
for world cinema.

Alliance Media is the international arm of the Alliance group — packaging, producing and financing stories that travel. From Melbourne, we take cinema born anywhere and carry it everywhere: Cannes, Berlin, Florida, Singapore and beyond.

Sister company to Mumbai's Alliance Productions India LLP, we operate where Australian craft meets global storytelling — co-producing award-winning work and developing an international slate spanning true-story drama, literary adaptation and period biography. Founder Deepak Tijori serves as an Industry Advisor on the West Australian Indian Film Council's Industry Advisory Council — part of the group's deepening roots in the Australian screen industry.

Echoes of Us

A film by Joe Rajan · Produced by Pooja Batra & Alliance Media

A heartfelt story of love, loss and second chances — two strangers, bound by what is no longer there. Deepak Tijori and Iulia Vantur lead a chamber piece that let absence speak louder than presence, and carried it from an independent set to the Croisette.
