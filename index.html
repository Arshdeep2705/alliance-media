The studio

We take cinema born anywhere and carry it everywhere.

Alliance Media is the international arm of the Alliance group — packaging, producing and financing stories that travel. From Melbourne: Cannes, Berlin, Florida, Singapore and beyond.

Sister company to Mumbai's Alliance Productions India LLP, we operate where Australian craft meets global storytelling — co-producing award-winning work and developing the group's next international feature, Defiant Nadia.

Founder Deepak Tijori serves as an Industry Advisor on the West Australian Indian Film Council's Industry Advisory Council — part of the group's deepening roots in the Australian screen industry.
