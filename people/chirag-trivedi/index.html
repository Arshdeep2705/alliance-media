Chief Financial Officer · Alliance Media Pty Ltd

Chirag Trivedi

CPA Australia Adelaide · Australia

The financial discipline behind the pictures — a career practice accountant bringing institutional-grade governance to independent film.

Chirag Trivedi is the Chief Financial Officer of Alliance Media Pty Ltd, responsible for the company's financial governance, production accounting, structuring and investor reporting. Every budget, cash-flow and financier statement across the Australian slate passes through his desk.

A member of CPA Australia with a Master of Commerce and Accounting, Chirag is also Principal & Partner of Holistic Business Developers, the Adelaide accounting practice he has built around complex tax, structuring and self-managed superannuation work for private clients and growing businesses.

He sits at the centre of the group's Australian-Indian footing — having served on the Australia India Business Council (SA) — which makes him a natural bridge for a company producing between Melbourne and Mumbai. For financiers and co-production partners, Chirag is the point of contact for due diligence, compliance and reporting on Defiant Nadia and the wider slate.
