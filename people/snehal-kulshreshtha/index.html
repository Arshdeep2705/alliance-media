Snehal Kulshreshtha

Press · Festivals · Partnerships Europe · Gulf · Asia · North America

Twenty years inside global luxury hospitality — now translating that relationship network into festival, financier and distributor access for Alliance.

Snehal Kulshreshtha leads PR and global outreach across the Alliance group. His two decades inside luxury hospitality were spent building exactly the kind of senior international relationships — across Europe, the Gulf, Asia and North America — that festival programmers, distribution executives and private financiers move within.

At Alliance he runs press, partner introductions and festival strategy, and is a primary point of contact for international co-production conversations and investor briefings — including on the upcoming international feature Defiant Nadia.
